About

I am an Applied AI Researcher working on large language models and multimodal learning. My work spans model pre-training and post-training, safety and alignment, evaluation, long-context processing, and dataset development. I have worked on AI applications in law, energy, and healthcare, with a focus on making models more reliable in real-world settings.

My research also extends to human motion understanding and generation, including motion representation learning, tokenization, quantization, text-to-motion generation, and learning across heterogeneous skeletal and motion schemas.

Language Models & Alignment

LLM pre-training and post-training, preference optimization, safety and robustness, long-context modeling, model evaluation, and high-quality datasets for specialized domains.
